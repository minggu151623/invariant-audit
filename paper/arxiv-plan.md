## Objective and candidate contribution

Produce a self-contained, evidence-audited English research manuscript, a
compiled PDF and minimal LaTeX source package suitable for the owner's arXiv
submission. arXiv posting is subject to moderation and is not peer review.
Do not equate a compiled package with scientific acceptance.

AI-proposed working framing: **When Perfect Validation Scores Reflect Test
Construction: An Audit of Semantic and State Invariant Benchmarks**.
This is an empirical methods case study of this repository's own constructions.
It is not a new validation algorithm, a survey, or a production benchmark.
Novelty and archival interest require a focused related-work comparison.

## Scientific work before manuscript completion

1. Lock E005 as exploratory analysis of previously inspected E001/E002 artifacts.
   Independently recompute confusion statistics, Brier and ECE without importing
   the old metric implementations. Verify hashes, counts and rounded agreement.
2. Measure score-group reliability, resolution and uncertainty; prove the exact
   finite Brier identity used here. Count distinct cluster statistic signatures
   without treating that count as an effective sample size.
3. Check case/method alignment, cross-split base/cluster disjointness, duplicated
   payload hashes and calibration-only lookup predictions. Preserve failures.
4. Separate the conditional mathematical argument (agreement on a finite test
   construction) from a claim that any validator must be perfect in the wild.
5. Compare directly with data-validation, oracle-problem and calibration papers.
   Explain what this case study adds and what is already known.
6. Complete an external check only under a separately locked, defensible design.
   E003-v2 remains exploratory and gated by independent acceptance. A stronger
   future claim needs sources/labels that were not selected using M1's contract.

An arXiv methods paper may present bounded negative findings without external
performance claims. The manuscript must explicitly justify that scope; if the
contribution is too slight after review, deepen the research before submission.
Do not manufacture an external result merely to fill a paper section.

## Manuscript and submission gates

- Trace every table/figure to immutable artifacts and a verified milestone.
- Distinguish previously locked results, exploratory reanalysis, and deductions.
- State positive=valid, binning and probability serialization precision.
- Include falsification conditions, limitations and reproduction commands.
- Independently review the analysis and manuscript; author tests are self-review.
- Verify references against primary sources, render the final PDF and inspect it.
- Obtain real author names/order, affiliations, owner-approved acknowledgements,
  category, license and any required endorsement before upload. Do not invent them.
- Prepare a compact source archive with no private/raw payload data or draft markers.
- Owner review and an explicit submission action are the final external step.
